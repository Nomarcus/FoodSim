A benchmark for TypeSafe's System One model, on its own: how fast it turns support-ticket text into a typed decision, and how often that decision is right. Every ticket is scored against a hand-written answer key the model never sees, and a keyword-rule baseline runs on the same text for contrast. The sorting simulation is on its own page.
